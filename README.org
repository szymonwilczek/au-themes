* About the project

=au-themes= is a collection of themes designed with care for autistic people, neurodivergent individuals, and anyone who struggles with visual fatigue.

The prefix *au-* refers to the chemical symbol for gold, recognized internationally as a symbol of autism acceptance.

* Why we made this

This project was created by me and my girlfriend. I spend long hours reading and writing code while living with progressive vision impairment, severe astigmatism, and photophobia. I know firsthand how exhausting and painful a poorly balanced screen can be: aggressive contrasts trigger sensory overload, overly saturated colors cause afterimages and headaches, and faint text forces the eyes to constantly strain to stay in focus.
My girlfriend has trained eye for color harmony, spatial balance, and natural tones. She stepped in to help me design palettes that feel organic, balanced, and visually restful.

* Who this is for

We made these themes for:

- *Autistic and neurodivergent individuals*: People who experience sensory overload, visual stress, or physical discomfort from flickering luminance boundaries and aggressive color palettes.
- *People with astigmatism and refractive issues*: The text is calibrated to avoid color fringing and chromatic blur, so your eye muscles do not tire themselves out hunting for sharp edges.
- *People sensitive to light (photophobia)*: Avoiding harsh pure whites and overly saturated tones that cause glare, visual afterimages, and headaches.
- *Engineers, writers, and night owls*: Anyone who spends long hours in front of a display and simply wants their eyes to feel relaxed at the end of the day.

* How they are designed

To make sure these themes are truly comfortable, we verified our choices with automated tests:

1. *Clear distinction*: Every syntax element has its own distinct color so you never have to guess what a token is, but neighboring tokens never clash or blur together.
2. *Readability without glare*: Text contrast is calculated using the APCA algorithm.
3. *Gentle on astigmatism*: The main reading text stays within low chroma bounds, which eliminates the annoying subpixel color halos seen on LCD panels.
4. *No glyph clumping*: Brackets and punctuation are slightly dialed back in contrast so words and identifiers stay clearly readable.
5. *Stable boundaries*: Adjacent syntax colors maintain a slight luminance difference to stop lines of code from appearing to jitter or vibrate when your eyes move across the screen.

* How to use

Load your preferred theme directly or via =use-package=:

#+begin_src elisp
  (use-package au-themes
    :ensure t
    :config
    (load-theme 'au-whispergrove-night t))
#+end_src

To quickly try out and switch between the themes:

#+begin_src text
  M-x au-themes-select
#+end_src
